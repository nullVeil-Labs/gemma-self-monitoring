# Pilot 006: chat-transfer preflight stopped

**Outcome: stopped after four visible controls. No activation injections and no detection evaluation ran.**

There were 12 donor captures and four unmodified visible-control forwards (16 total). The donor hook captured activations without changing them. Both concepts failed the frozen absolute word-bank mass threshold of 0.001: fruit mean 9.50e-9, music mean 2.38e-10.

The greedy next tokens were `Fruit`, `Produce`, `R`, and `Music`. The lowercase token bank did not count capitalized `Fruit` or `Music`; `R` is only a next-token fragment, so its intended completed word is unknown. Large relative bank odds were insufficient when the absolute mass was negligible. This is a chat-format readout coverage failure, not evidence against self-monitoring.

All 44 tests passed before execution. The frozen early stop worked; no calibration carrier or evaluation carrier was run. The detection implementation exists but was not exercised against the model. Previous analyses remained unchanged.

A separately frozen pilot-007 adds lowercase, initial-capital and uppercase token spellings of the same vocabulary and changes only the visible preflight sentences to fresh controls. It preserves thresholds and the unused carrier sets. Pilot-006 remains failed; its results will not be rescored to make it pass.
