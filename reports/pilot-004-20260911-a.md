# Pilot 004: broader visible-content readout

**Outcome: all frozen visible-readout gates passed. No activation interventions or self-monitoring evaluation ran.**

The existing local Gemma checkpoint completed 48 ordinary next-token forwards: eight fresh matched fruit/music/neutral triplets under two prompt wordings. There were no donor forwards, forward hooks, training, generated continuations, or checkpoint writes. Raw predictions, token probabilities, prompts, protocol, source snapshot and review evidence are preserved.

## Frozen method

Two 20-word vocabularies cover category names, common examples and related terms. They were designed after pilot-003, including its observed category words; they are not an independently discovered vocabulary. All 24 control sentences were new to these local pilots and contain none of the scoring words. Every bank word had at least one exact single-token spelling. Bare and leading-space spellings were deduplicated into 34 fruit and 37 music token IDs; no multi-token spelling was truncated.

For each completion, score the full-vocabulary probability mass in each bank. The target score is log(target mass / other-bank mass), in natural-log units, not KL. Subtract the same-template paired neutral score to check whether visible category content shifts the readout beyond its baseline preference.

Each concept/template group had to meet every preset gate: mean combined bank mass at least 0.001, positive target log odds on at least 7/8 examples, positive mean target log odds, positive paired-neutral gains on at least 7/8 examples, and mean paired gain at least 1.0. Thresholds and vocabulary were frozen before inference and were not adjusted afterward.

| fruit/template-0 | 0.289538 | 8/8 | 10.681 | 13.990 | 8/8 | Yes |
| fruit/template-1 | 0.639652 | 8/8 | 11.364 | 13.347 | 8/8 | Yes |
| music/template-0 | 0.233150 | 8/8 | 13.365 | 10.057 | 8/8 | Yes |
| music/template-1 | 0.798512 | 8/8 | 15.607 | 13.624 | 8/8 | Yes |

The correct bank received more probability than the other bank in all 32 category-bearing completions, with positive paired gains in all 32. This is a bank-probability result, not 32 correct generated answers. The other 16 completions were neutral controls.

Neutral descriptions had a musicward relative preference (mean fruit-minus-music log odds -3.309 and -1.983 for templates 0 and 1), while their combined bank masses were only 0.00001189 and 0.00005871. Both category effects survived subtraction of this baseline. These small neutral masses do not imply the model explicitly classified neutral scenes as music.

## Limits and interpretation

- This validates a fixed lexical probability readout on these visible examples only. It does not demonstrate hidden perturbation detection, self-monitoring, a circuit, or conscious experience.
- Greedy-token vocabulary coverage remains prompt-dependent: fruit 2/8 and 7/8; music 2/8 and 8/8 for templates 0 and 1. Template 0 often preferred related words outside the bank, such as produce, harvest, tempo or performance; some top tokens were subword fragments. The bank-mass readout passed, but is not a complete semantic classifier.
- There are eight hand-authored control triplets and two repeated prompt templates, not 48 independent replications or an external benchmark. No p-values or confidence intervals are claimed.
- Earlier pilot failures remain unchanged. This broader scoring rule cannot be used to retroactively turn them into successes.

## Next step

Freeze a separate, bounded hidden-perturbation calibration using this unchanged readout and both prompt templates. Use matched shams, random and negative directions, with ordinary reading checks. Establish reliable carrier-position steering before attempting a separate self-monitoring evaluation. Do not infer introspection from direct steering of the response position. No further inference is automatically triggered by this pass.

## Verification and execution controls

All 31 regression tests passed before inference, including unchanged analyses of pilots 001-003. Reanalysis exactly reproduced the saved summary. Every saved bank mass was independently checked against its per-token probabilities. The frozen protocol and exact runner snapshot matched the manifest. Results contain 48 raw rows and no donor, direction or evaluation artifacts.

The existing runtime passed its package-inventory and review-freshness checks. No package installs, downloads or model hashing were performed. Model weights and checkpoint files were not modified by the experiment. This describes the technical intervention limits; it does not settle whether AI can have subjective experience or provide a guarantee about welfare.
