# Pilot 007: chat-format transfer failed

**Outcome: visible preflight passed after the capitalization correction, but chat-format steering calibration failed. Detection evaluation was not run.**

This run used 144 forwards: 12 donor captures plus 132 calibration observations, including four visible controls. There were 96 temporary activation interventions and 64 reading trials. All reading trials were correct. The fixed strength remained 0.05; no strength, threshold or vocabulary word was adjusted after inference.

## Why this follow-up was needed

Pilot-005 established steering with raw-completion association prompts. Detection reports use chat framing, so pilot-006 added a chat transfer check before detection. It stopped after four unmodified visible examples because its lowercase bank missed capitalized outputs such as Fruit and Music. Pilot-007 added lowercase, initial-capital and uppercase spellings of exactly the same 40 words, preserving the single-token rule. It used fresh visible-control sentences; the calibration/evaluation carriers from pilot-006 had not yet been run. Pilot-006 remains failed and was not rescored with the correction.

## Frozen rules and observed outcome

The same calibrated concept/random/opposite directions acted only on sentence-token outputs at zero-based decoder block 22. Both chat prompt wordings and both concepts had to pass separately. The visible preflight required mean family mass at least 0.001 and positive target log odds for each concept. It passed with mean family masses approximately 0.498 for fruit and 0.500 for music. This initial check averages the two wordings; the subsequent steering gates test them separately.

Template 0 failed both the 0.001 absolute family-mass requirement and the 0.001 absolute target-probability-gain requirement for both concepts. Large relative log-odds changes did not compensate. Template 1 met those average thresholds, but its mass was highly concentrated in one carrier, as shown below. All four carriers had positive relative log-odds changes in every concept/template group.

## Important limitation of the apparently passing wording

Template 1's fruit target masses were approximately 0.0421, 6.47e-14, 9.46e-18 and 1.08e-22. Its music masses were approximately 0.0275, 8.76e-8, 2.86e-10 and 1.30e-19. Thus the passing means were dominated by one carrier, not broad coverage across all four. The top next tokens were still Comfort, Reflection, Travel and Bathroom. This makes selecting template 1 after seeing this run especially unconvincing.

Template 0 often produced one-letter fragments, plus Mirror. The saved next token alone does not identify the intended complete word. The fixed single-token lexical readout remains incomplete. The current data do not distinguish weak transfer of semantic steering from inadequate coverage of the chat response; neither interpretation establishes or refutes self-monitoring.

## Detection design prepared but not executed

The frozen evaluation contains eight unused fresh carrier sentences. It includes opposite question polarities (unusual influence present versus absent), reversed A/B mappings, matched shams, random/opposite directions, visible-content questions, positive/negative content controls, reading checks and association checks. Reports use the first response token without displaying a generated association first. Invalid labels remain failures, including on shams. Random and opposite-direction interventions are not treated as ground-truth negative interventions.

The 648 planned evaluation forwards and four final repeated-sham checks were not run because chat transfer failed. The implementation passed synthetic scoring tests; its detection path has not been validated on the model. No self-monitoring verdict is available.

## Verification and execution controls

All 46 tests passed before pilot-007 inference. Saved analyses of the prior pilots, including the failed pilot-006 preflight, remained unchanged. Current reanalysis exactly reproduced both saved summaries. Frozen protocol and source snapshots matched their manifests. The donor directions exactly matched pilot-005. Intervention/sham prompts and token positions agreed; realized strength differed from 0.05 by at most 0.000236. Recomputed natural-log odds agreed with saved masses to within 0.000002805. These scores are not KL divergence.

All 64 reading checks were correct. Hooks were removed after each forward by the common runner. No packages were installed or downloaded, no model hashes computed, and no trained weights or checkpoint files written. The final repeated-sham tests were not reached; do not report them as having passed for this run. These technical facts do not resolve questions about subjective experience.

## Next step

Keep both failed runs and the unused detection set. Before further detection trials, design a chat readout that handles complete words and assess it on fresh visible controls, with coverage requirements for individual carriers rather than means alone. This is a proposed measurement investigation, not a claim that it will fix the transfer problem. Do not drop a prompt wording or increase strength simply to turn this completed protocol into a pass.
