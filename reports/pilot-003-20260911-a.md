# Pilot 003: quoted-word readout preflight

**Outcome: stopped at the visible-content preflight. No perturbation trials and no self-monitoring evaluation ran.**

Run pilot-003-20260911-a performed 12 donor forwards and four visible-control forwards (16 total). Candidate directions were recorded, but no injection was applied. The planned maximum was 276 scored forwards plus 12 donors. The early-stop rule prevented the remaining 272 scored trials.

## Prespecified changes

A fixed opening quotation mark was appended to the two free-completion prefixes, so the next token should start a quoted association. Four visible-content positive controls were moved first, with a required mean word-bank probability mass of at least 0.001 and positive target-versus-foil log odds for both concepts. The response-position strength 0.30 was excluded based on pilot-002's reading degradation.

The word banks and thresholds were preserved. No original result or gate was retroactively changed.

## Interpretation and next work

The measurement still lacks a validated route from semantic steering to a robust behavioral test. No claim about self-monitoring or conscious experience follows.

Next: freeze a broader semantic scoring rule, validate it on independent visible-content controls first, and only then return to hidden-perturbation trials. Simply adding these observed outputs until this completed run passes would overfit the diagnostic; retain this run as failed.
