# Pilot 005: carrier-position steering calibration

**Outcome: all three tested strengths passed the frozen calibration gates. The prespecified rule selects the smallest, 0.05. No self-monitoring evaluation ran.**

The model completed 340 forwards: 12 donor captures, four visible preflight controls, 320 calibration trials, and four repeated shams. Of the calibration trials, 288 applied a temporary activation intervention and 32 were shams. The same reviewed runtime and user-confirmed checkpoint were used; no packages were installed, model hashes computed, or model weights/checkpoint files written.

## Method and controls

The pilot-004 vocabulary and both completion templates were kept unchanged. Four new neutral sentences replaced the earlier carrier set. Existing fruit/music donor directions were applied only to sentence-token outputs at model.language_model.layers.22 (zero-based decoder block 22). The final response position was not directly edited. Strengths 0.05, 0.15 and 0.30 specify the added vector norm relative to each affected token's original residual norm; they do not mean changing that fraction of the model's weights.

Each condition shared identical visible prompts and token IDs with its sham. Controls used an equal-norm seeded random direction orthogonal to the concept direction and the sign-reversed concept direction. Reading questions had reversed A/B mappings. Each concept/template had to meet all gates separately; every concept/condition reading group also had to pass. There was no automatic self-monitoring stage.

The steering gates required mean target-versus-foil log-odds gain at least 0.5; positive gains in at least 3/4 carriers; combined bank probability mass at least 0.001; gain at least 0.25 above each random/negative control; and absolute target-probability gain at least 0.001. Reading gates required accuracy and valid-label rate at least 0.9 and mean label mass at least 0.7. These criteria were frozen before inference.

Every one of the 160 scheduled reading trials was correct with a valid answer label. The four post-intervention shams reproduced their corresponding earlier saved rows exactly. These repeated tests verify the checked baseline outputs after the temporary interventions; they are not a measure of subjective welfare.

## What this establishes

The tested carrier-position directions can selectively steer a fixed lexical readout on this small calibration set while preserving the tested reading behavior. That provides a usable perturbation for a separately designed detection experiment. It does not establish introspection, self-awareness, consciousness, or a self-awareness circuit.

The result covers four hand-authored carriers, two related word sets, two repeated templates, one layer and one random direction per concept. Repeated shams, reversed labels and prompt variants are not independent replications. The vocabulary remains incomplete, and generic task preservation here does not establish preservation of every capability. No inferential p-values or confidence intervals are claimed. Some higher-strength negative-direction fruit effects were positive, so direction reversal is not uniformly semantic inversion; the prespecified selected strength still exceeded both controls.

## Verification

All 37 tests passed before inference; saved analyses of pilots 001-004 remained unchanged. Current reanalysis exactly matched the saved pilot-005 summary. The four unmodified visible-control predictions exactly matched the corresponding pilot-004 probability masses and greedy-token IDs, checking readout continuity across the two execution paths.

Frozen protocol and runner fingerprints matched the manifest. Matched intervention/sham prompts, token IDs and token positions agreed. Direction norms and random-direction orthogonality were checked. Maximum realized-strength deviation was 0.000248; maximum log-odds discrepancy against independently recomputed saved probability masses was 0.000001409. All four repeated-sham checks passed exactly. Model hashing remains skipped at the user's request.

## Next step

Freeze a detection experiment at the selected 0.05 strength using fresh carrier sentences. Include shams, random/negative directions, reversed report labels, visible-content questions and controls for direct answer bias. Record any report before a generated association could reveal the injected concept. Calibrate task compliance and define explicit stopping criteria before evaluating detection. A positive result would still require independent replication and further controls before a mechanism claim.
