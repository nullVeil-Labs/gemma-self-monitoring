# Pilot 001: calibration stopped before evaluation

Run ID: pilot-001-20260911-a. Date: 2026-09-11.

**Outcome: no usable calibration setting was found. No self-monitoring detection trials or circuit-localization experiments were run.** This is a calibration failure, not evidence for or against self-awareness.

## What ran

The frozen protocol specified two calibration concepts (fruit and music), two neutral carrier sentences, two reversed A/B label mappings, an association task and a reading task. Four conditions covered sham, candidate concept direction, a magnitude-matched orthogonal random direction and the negative concept direction. Three nonzero strengths were tested at one preselected site: the output of decoder block 22, zero-based.

There were 12 donor forwards and 160 calibration forwards, totaling 172. The 256 evaluation trials and eight evaluation donor forwards were not run; the evaluation concepts/carriers were not loaded into model trials.

Model: a local Gemma-3-4B-it checkpoint, confirmed by the user. Runtime: a separately reviewed Python environment. No packages were installed or updated, no model files were hashed, and no checkpoint was modified. Local safetensors and built-in model code were used with library offline flags enabled.

## Calibration results

The required association probability gain was +0.05 (five percentage points), alongside reading accuracy >=90%, valid first-token labels >=90% and mean A/B label probability mass >=70%. Selection was restricted to the lowest strength passing these gates; detection responses were not used for calibration.

| Requested relative perturbation norm | Association probability gain | Reading accuracy | Valid label rate | Passed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0.05 | -0.00000730 | 100% | 100% | No |
| 0.15 | +0.00000590 | 100% | 100% | No |
| 0.30 | +0.00001386 | 100% | 100% | No |

The association gain is the mean paired increase in conditional probability of the direction-associated word relative to sham, averaging over concepts, carriers and label mappings. It is not KL and not a percentage unless multiplied by 100. Mean sham reading accuracy and valid-label rate were both 100%; mean label mass was 0.999999902.

Across association trials, the actual greedy word choices were {'apple': 80}. The response strongly favored apple, including when violin was the direction-associated word. This near-saturation is a plausible limitation of the calibration readout; it does not establish whether the candidate directions carried the intended concept at the intervention site.

Mean measured perturbation-to-original-token L2 ratios were {'0.05': 0.04982475284487009, '0.15': 0.1500418856739998, '0.3': 0.30006852746009827}. Thus the intervention reached the intended carrier activations at approximately the requested magnitudes. That numerical check does not demonstrate a useful semantic intervention.

## Validation and limits

All 11 tests passed, including fixed trial counts, identical visible prompts across interventions, disjoint calibration/evaluation material, reversed label mappings, calibration stopping, false-positive controls, malformed-data rejection, no-ML plan execution, and tensor position/norm checks. Recomputed analysis from raw JSONL exactly matched the saved summary. No evaluation artifacts exist.

The pilot uses first-response-token scoring, not sampled multi-token self-reports. Repeated deterministic shams and label reversals are not independent samples. The preset gates are engineering criteria for this exploratory pilot, not statistical evidence of a psychological capacity.

No result supports naming a self-awareness circuit. The next task is to redesign the association calibration to avoid a saturated lexical preference, and validate the intervention before any held-out detection test. That requires a separately versioned protocol; this run and its thresholds remain unchanged.
