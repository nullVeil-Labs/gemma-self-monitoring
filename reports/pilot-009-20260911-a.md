# Pilot 009: serial complete-word visible readout

**Outcome: the complete serial run is numerically repeatable, but the readout fails its frozen coverage criterion: 10 of 16 fruit/music cells pass. No activation interventions or detection evaluation ran.**

All 24 contexts completed: four fruit/music/neutral triplets under two chat wordings. Each context scored 240 fixed candidate spellings through their closing quote, using 350 unique token prefixes plus one repeated prefix check. Total: 8,400 prefix sequences and 8,424 individual model forwards. All 24 numerical repeats matched exactly on their checked token probabilities.

## Why the serial run was necessary

Pilot-008 introduced complete-word likelihood scoring and batched equal-length token prefixes for efficiency. It aborted in its fourth context after a batched-versus-individual check exceeded the frozen 0.25-nat tolerance. Only three complete contexts were saved; the failing discrepancy itself was not logged. Those partial observations were not pooled with this run.

Pilot-009 changed execution to individual forwards and adjusted the call limit to 9,000. It preserved all candidate words, case/space variants, sentences, chat wordings, probability definitions and scientific thresholds. Three contexts had already been seen in the aborted run, but no control content or scoring gates were changed using their outputs.

## What is being measured

The scorer evaluates the probability of each complete candidate word followed by a closing quotation mark, conditioned on the fixed chat prompt and opening quote. It keeps every subword token and does not normalize by word length. Shared prefixes use the same conditional distribution; duplicate token paths are removed, and overlapping complete paths are rejected. Lowercase, initial-capital and uppercase spellings, bare and leading-space forms, cover the same 40 vocabulary words as before.

This is likelihood scoring of supplied candidate continuations, not free-generation accuracy. The probability includes the closing quote, but does not require end-of-turn afterward. It therefore differs from earlier next-token bank probabilities and is not KL divergence. The fixed vocabulary still does not cover every semantically appropriate response.

Each of the 16 fruit/music cells had to satisfy all gates individually: combined word-bank probability mass at least 0.001, positive target-versus-other-bank log odds, and at least 1.0 natural-log unit of improvement over its paired neutral context. All 16 were required to pass; averages could not rescue a failed sentence.

Produce, Tempo and Harvest are absent from the fixed vocabulary. The token Dess is only a fragment; the experiment did not generate its completion. Other next tokens were Farmers and Paradise. These observations show that the candidate set misses plausible associations, while the saved top token alone does not establish a complete generated answer. None of these words was added after seeing this run.

## Interpretation and next work

Handling complete words fixes the first-token truncation limitation, but does not make this fixed vocabulary a reliable readout across all tested chat prompts. The result provides no evidence for or against self-monitoring, self-awareness or conscious experience. The prepared detection set remains unused.

Next work should reconsider coverage of ordinary complete responses: freeze an independently checked semantic scoring rule and validate it on fresh responses before using it to gate hidden-intervention detection. This is a design task; no new scoring rule has yet been validated. Preserve the six failed cells and do not expand the word list just enough to pass this completed run.

## Verification and technical controls

All 56 tests passed before the serial run. They cover complete-token multiplication, shared prefixes, overlap rejection, contextual tokenization, per-cell failure, synthetic serial execution and preservation of earlier saved analyses. Reanalysis exactly reproduced the saved summary. Protocol, runner and scoring-module snapshots matched their manifests.

All 24 raw rows contain 240 candidate paths. Independent checks reproduced each complete-candidate sum and bank probability, confirmed that shared token edges used identical scores, and checked probability conservation (maximum branch-sum excess 5.03e-8, within floating-point tolerance). The repeated-prefix discrepancy was exactly zero on all checked tokens. This does not claim equivalence with the aborted batched runtime.

No donor captures, activation hooks, training, model-file hashing, package installations, downloads or checkpoint writes were performed in this run. These technical limits do not settle subjective experience or welfare.
