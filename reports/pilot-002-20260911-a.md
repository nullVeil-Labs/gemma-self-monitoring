# Pilot 002: undisplayed lexical readout

**Outcome: direction-dependent changes in word-bank probabilities, but no setting passed the full calibration gate. No self-monitoring evaluation ran.**

Run pilot-002-20260911-a completed 324 scored forwards plus 12 donor forwards (336 total). It reused only previously seen fruit/music calibration material, the existing model and the reviewed runtime. No downloads, package changes, model hashing, training or checkpoint writes occurred.

## Method

The first pilot's explicit apple/violin choice was replaced by a free-completion prefix. The readout used predeclared word banks: fruit = orange, banana, lemon, grape; music = guitar, drum, cello, harp. These words were absent from the visible free-association prompt and disjoint from donor words.

The same concept, negative and magnitude-matched random directions were applied either to the carrier-sentence positions or directly to the final response-prediction position of block 22. The latter is an output-path diagnostic, not evidence of introspection. Reading checks retained reversed A/B labels.

The primary score is paired change in target-family versus foil-family natural-log odds, calculated with logsumexp over the designated tokens. This is not KL. Absolute family probability mass is also required to prevent a large ratio between negligible alternatives from passing.

All six settings failed the preset minimum family mass of 0.001 for each concept. Response strength 0.30 also degraded reading accuracy/format; it was excluded from the subsequent protocol before that protocol ran.

The full raw summary includes random and negative controls. Concept-direction effects generally exceeded those controls in this small fixed dataset, but no significance or generalization claim is made.

## Readout diagnosis

All 16 sham free-association trials predicted either a colon or “ that” as the next token. Thus the nominal word readout was being measured at a position where the model preferred punctuation or a grammatical continuation. Visible-content positive controls also put little mass on the word banks. These observations motivated the separately frozen pilot-003; pilot-002 results and gates were not changed.

The results show an effect on relative word probabilities under these interventions, not reliable verbal identification of an internal concept or self-monitoring.

## Validation and artifacts

All 20 tests passed before this run. After the subsequent implementation change, all 24 tests passed, including exact reanalysis of pilot-001 and this run. Recomputed pilot-002 analysis equals the saved summary. No evaluation files exist.
